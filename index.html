What period does the 2027 All Access Pass cover?

The pass covers eligible Boon Tax Educators live webinars offered January 1 through December 31, 2027.

Can I purchase the pass after January 1?

Yes. The pass remains available after the early-bird period at the regular price. Purchasers receive access to the remaining scheduled live webinars for 2027.

Are newly scheduled webinars included?

Yes. Additional eligible live webinars added to the 2027 schedule are included at no additional charge.

Does the pass include self-study courses?

No. The pass covers the 2027 live webinar program and associated recordings and materials. Separately offered self-study courses are not included.

Is the All Access Pass automatically renewed?

No. The 2027 pass is a one-time purchase covering the 2027 calendar year, not an automatically renewing subscription.
